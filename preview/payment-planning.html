Payment Planning & Liquidity Platform

A liquidity-management tool for a multi-entity industrial group: it imports SAP open items, matches payables against receivables, optimizes payments by due date and cash-discount window, and runs a role-based approval workflow — 100% on-premise.

01 · Core function

The matching & planning engine

At the heart is a four-step pipeline that turns raw SAP exports into a ready-to-approve payment proposal. The same core logic runs headless (batch) and behind the UI.

1

Import

Read SAP open-item exports for accounts payable & receivable; normalize vendors, dates and amounts.

2

Match

Four-step chain that links creditor invoices to the matching debit/credit notes per business partner.

3

Optimize

Net the balances, then rank by due date and open cash-discount window to maximize savings within available liquidity.

4

Approve

Build the payment proposal and route it through Preparer → Management → Accounting, with a full audit trail.

Inputs
  • SAP open items (AP + AR), .xlsx
  • Vendor & terms master data
  • Current bank balances
Outputs
  • Prioritized payment proposal
  • Cash-discount savings report
  • Bank / SEPA payment file

Family Meal Planner

A full-stack app for planning the family's week: pick recipes onto a weekly plan, and it auto-builds the shopping list by aggregating every ingredient. It was also an early experiment in orchestrating a multi-agent build — from idea to working product.

ReactViteExpress NodeMulti-agent build

Personal project, not publicly hosted. The screens below are a faithful rebuild of the UI with example recipes — so you can see how it looks and works.

01 · Core function

Plan → recipes → shopping list

The core turns a week of chosen recipes into one consolidated shopping list: it scales each recipe to the family size, merges duplicate ingredients and groups them by aisle.

1

Pick

Choose recipes from the library onto each day and meal slot of the week.

2

Scale

Scale ingredient quantities to the number of servings per household.

3

Aggregate

Merge the same ingredient across recipes into a single line item.

4

List

Group by store aisle into a checkable shopping list, ready on the phone.

Inputs
  • Recipe library with ingredients
  • Weekly plan (day × meal)
  • Household size
Outputs
  • Filled weekly meal plan
  • Scaled per-recipe ingredients
  • Consolidated shopping list

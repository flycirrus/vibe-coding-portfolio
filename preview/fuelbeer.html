FuelBeer — Fuel Companion

A pilot's fuel companion in a clean mobile web app: enter the leg, and it computes the full fuel breakdown — trip, contingency, alternate, final reserve and extra — into a single block-fuel figure, with the whole calculation laid out so nothing is a black box.

01 · Core function

The fuel-calculation core

Given the leg and a few parameters, the core assembles every regulatory fuel component and sums them to a block-fuel figure — transparent and reproducible.

1

Inputs

Trip time, burn rate, alternate and the reserve policy for the leg.

2

Components

Compute trip, contingency, alternate, final reserve, taxi and any extra fuel.

3

Sum

Add to block fuel and show the full breakdown, component by component.

4

Check

Flag against tank capacity and highlight how much margin is left.

Inputs
  • Trip time & fuel burn rate
  • Alternate & holding requirement
  • Reserve / contingency policy
Outputs
  • Full component breakdown
  • Block fuel figure
  • Tank-capacity margin check

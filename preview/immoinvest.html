ImmoNavigator — Investment Dashboard

A personal real-estate investment dashboard: it tracks a multi-property portfolio, computes cashflow, equity and yield per property, models sale scenarios, and simulates a "path to a million" strategy over 10 years. Private tool — the figures below are representative example values.

HTML/CSS/JSDashboardScenario simulator NetlifyPersonal finance

Personal finances. The values, yields and property labels below are representative example data — this preview shows the structure and the UI, not the real portfolio.

01 · Core function

Portfolio → projection engine

The dashboard consolidates every property, rolls up the portfolio, and projects it forward against a target — the same model powers the overview, the valuations and the strategy simulator.

1

Collect

Per property: purchase price, market value, equity, loan terms, rent and costs.

2

Compute

Cashflow, yield and equity per property, then the consolidated portfolio position.

3

Project

Model value growth and repayment forward toward the €1M target — the "path to a million".

4

Simulate

Play what-if sale and refinancing scenarios and see the effect on the goal.

Inputs
  • Property master data & valuations
  • Loan & interest terms
  • Rent, costs & assumptions
Outputs
  • Portfolio overview & cashflow
  • Per-property yield & equity
  • "Path to a million" projection
